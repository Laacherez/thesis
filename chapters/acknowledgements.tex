Hehe, what an adventure! It's pretty crazy how much I grew and changed once I got to know you all. I sort of learnt the meaning of friendship and companionship inside of these green walls. I did have an idea of the thing, just not the full picture yet. Of course, not everything came from all of you, and I like to think my determination and ambition I found by myself. But for the rest- Gagou's resilience, Ahmad's gentleness, Quentin's compartimentalisation, Irina's willpower, Guirec's verve, Romain's charisma, Andrea's perfectionism, Ariane's calm, Marco's kindness, Camila's empathy, Elodie's unstoppable force, Gaspard's swiftness, Vicky's maturity, Hugo's knowledge and Ouzo's wisdom- I do believe I learnt a little bit from each of you. This is only how I percieve you, and the aspects of you which I draw inspiration from. I feel like a Harlequin of a person, sometimes, having gathered so many little bits from such colorful people. I will miss you, so much, and I promise I will get over it and perpetuate the LOMA's traditional openness and care for each other, wherever I go.
